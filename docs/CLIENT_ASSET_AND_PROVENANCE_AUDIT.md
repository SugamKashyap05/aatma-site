## 2. How to use the client images without weakening the homepage

- **Hero:** Use an actual client agarwood/forest photograph as a single image plane with restrained motion, careful focal-point cropping, and a text-safe overlay. The best clear process photo is portrait-oriented (2252×4000); the chip close-up and green-tree shots are 1024×768 and 1000×800. They are excellent section/gallery images but are not high-resolution wide landscape originals. Ask the client for a high-resolution landscape agarwood-tree/forest image if the hero must fill wide desktop screens sharply. Do not fabricate depth by slicing one flat image into false foreground/background cutouts; subtle single-plane parallax is safer unless a designer prepares credible masks.
- **Plantation and NDF:** Use the two green-tree photos and the plantation collage, with verified client captions. Keep the numerical claims tied to the brief/NDF source, not to the visual alone.
- **Agar materials/products:** Use the chips and bottles in an “Agarwood and products” area. Confirm bottle labels before using product names; do not add prices, purity/quality statements, or chemical descriptions from the photograph.
- **Association/history/press:** Use the 2016 banner and meeting/delegation pictures as a dated archive. Preserve the event/date/place shown on the client banner or caption. Use generic roles (e.g. “AATMA delegation”) rather than full names in line with the client’s privacy/name instruction.
- **Press archive:** Use the clippings as client-supplied primary evidence. For accessible web copy, transcribe the relevant story accurately and cite the newspaper, publication date, page if legible, and “client-supplied clipping.” Avoid carrying over neighboring unrelated articles.
- **WhatsApp screenshots:** Keep as internal evidence. Extract original photos and approved captions instead of publishing full private chats.
- **Proceedings book:** Add as an archival book only after checking page order, title/date, scan legibility, rights/permission, and the participant-list pages. Provide the scanned original with accessible title/description; do not invent a text layer or imply the 2001 proceedings are current advice.
